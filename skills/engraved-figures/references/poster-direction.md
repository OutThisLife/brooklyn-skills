# Poster and redaction direction

Inspect [approved-poster.png](approved-poster.png). The useful qualities are the substantial scientific figures, distribution across a cyclic composition, quiet serif hierarchy, lower-column density, and sparse opaque blackouts. Do not copy its particular subject into unrelated work.

## Prompt scaffold

> Create a scientific technical plate about [subject]. Use the supplied reference for composition and line character. Clean white paper, black and neutral gray ink, fine engraved wireframes, stippled points, serif typography, hairline rules. [List distinct figures, their relative sizes, positions and meaningful relationships.] [Exact copy and labels.] All numerical plots must use supplied data; abstract geometry is schematic. No icons, rounded cards, glossy 3D shading, decorative gradients, stamps, document codes or aged-paper dirt. Preserve readable labels at the intended output size.

If redactions are requested, enumerate the exact labels or names that must be hidden. Specify that bars conceal the letters with no white text over them. For an intentionally irregular effect, identify a few complete labels, a few words inside phrases and occasional partial words. Do not hide every caption by default.

## Revision lessons

- “More figures” means more substantial scientific art, not additional boxes or labels.
- “Old-school” can be achieved with monochrome linework, stippling and typography. Do not default to sepia, stains, distressed paper or stamps.
- “Classified” or “released files” can mean implied withholding. A clean document with real blackouts often captures that intent better than literal institutional props.
- A request for less cheese is a targeted removal of theatrical details. Preserve the approved figures, framing and density.
- Partial redaction should obscure the chosen portion; a narrow line through a readable name does not satisfy “the name must never be visible.”

For separate figure exports, retain the approved perspective and silhouette. Omit branding and captions unless needed. Report actual dimensions and identify generative re-creations as such. Keep the full approved composition alongside the individual assets.
